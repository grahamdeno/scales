# Scales 2.1 plan: new times, Settings with Restore, then the website

Decided by Graham on 29 October 2026; planned by Opus; built by Sonnet unless a step says otherwise.
**One release.** Everything below, app and website, ships together in one pull request from the branch
`release-2.1`, after Graham has seen the screenshots. Nothing is merged or goes live before then.

## Decisions

- **Times:** 20 s or less on screen is a skip. 30 s or more is thought about. Between 20 and 30 s is seen: it comes back
  in 2 days and changes no counts (same rule as today's 7 to 11 s band). Edits and notes still count as worked on,
  whatever the time. Idle over 5 minutes still counts as seen.
- **Settings exists, and Restore lives there, not in the library.** The library is for thoughts. Settings is for the
  app: your data, help, privacy, support, version. Back up moves from the library foot into Settings too.
- **How you get there:** Meet (one thought, nothing else on screen) → pull up or tap the strip → the library →
  a settings icon (a gear, 44 x 44, bone at 70%) in the library header, left of "+ New" → Settings, a plum page that
  slides in from the right, with "Your thoughts" (back chevron) at the top left to return. Meet stays clean; Settings
  is two taps away, which is fine for something used rarely.
- **Privacy in the app:** Apple's guideline 5.1.1(i) requires a privacy policy link in App Store Connect and "within
  the app in an easily accessible manner". Settings carries it.

## Restore: what it does (for Graham)

- Adds thoughts this phone does not have. Never changes or removes one that is here.
- A thought you deleted on this phone comes back if the backup has it and the backup does not list it as deleted.
  Choosing a backup is asking for what is in it, and this is how an accidental delete is undone. Restoring an older
  daily file can therefore bring back thoughts deleted since that day; delete them again if so.
- Scales 1 thoughts inside a backup arrive 5 a day, as on the first import.

## Website wording changes that come with this plan

Support, "How do I move to a new phone?": "Install Scales on the new phone. Open Your thoughts, tap the gear, choose
Restore from a backup, and pick the latest file in iCloud Drive, Scales. Restoring adds what's missing and never
overwrites."

Privacy and Support pages are the ones Settings links to; their addresses must stay `/privacy` and `/support`.

## Watch after shipping (Graham)

The visit log started on 29 October under the old lines (7 s and 11 s) and continues under the new ones; each entry
says which lines were in force. With 20 s as the line, most quick reads will count as skips. Each skip brings a thought back tomorrow and sets it one
step bolder (up to 4 steps). If, after a week, nearly every thought is bold and back daily, the line is doing more than
intended; step 10 settles it with the numbers.

## 2.2.0 (on `release-2.2`, not shipped)

Decided by Graham on 29 October 2026. Built by Sonnet; the restore handling of the new field awaits an Opus review.

- **Library sheet** rests on the strip when shut, rises from it and settles back into it (it used to park below the screen).
- **Icons:** Pistachio, Plum and Bone (`scripts/icons.py`); Automatic follows Light and Dark. Chosen under Settings, App icon, in the iPhone app only. Needs the TestFlight build that starts when `ios/**` merges.
- **Done and Keep** are plain pistachio words, not pills. Edit and Write fields grow to fit; the form scrolls as a whole.
- **Website copy:** children section and summary cards removed; contact box after the answers; hero and closing lines no longer repeat.
- **Bookmark.** A toggle at the top right of Edit (applied with Done, left alone by Cancel). `bookmarked` is a timestamp or null on each thought. It never changes when a thought comes back, its weight, or its rotation. It does not count as work.
  - **Bookmarked filter** (with the count, "Bookmarked · 3") and **New** filter (added in the last 14 days, newest first, by the date made, which editing never changes). Filters scroll sideways with a fade on the right edge.
  - A small mark beside bookmarked thoughts in every list.
  - **Review rule:** a thought opened from the Bookmarked list is a review. It comes up on screen (put-away ones too); moving on from it changes nothing: no due date, no skips, no thinks, no visit. It is logged with `kind: "review"` so the times review (step 10) leaves it out.
  - Restore carries `bookmarked`; an existing thought keeps its own. A 2.1 build restoring a 2.2 backup drops bookmarks.
- Version 2.2.0 (core, worker cache, both `core.js?v=`).
