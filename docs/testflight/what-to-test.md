Thanks for trying Scales.

1. Write a thought you half believe. Add two or three more.
2. Open one to edit it and tap Argue with it. This needs Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later, turned on in Settings).
3. Once you have a few thoughts, look at Tensions and Insights.
4. Come back over a few days. Skip some, sit with others, put one away.
