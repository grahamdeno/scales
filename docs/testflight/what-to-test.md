Thanks for trying Scales. This build (2.33.1) asks three things of you:

1. Larger Text: in iPhone Settings, Display & Brightness, Text Size, make the text large. Scales should follow it, with nothing cut off.
2. Write a thought and tap Keep. Then swipe Scales away in the app switcher and open it again. The thought should still be there.
3. On a fresh start, tapping the question "What's on your mind?" should put the cursor in the box.

Then use it as usual: skip some thoughts, sit with others, put one away, try Argue with it (needs Apple Intelligence).

Tell me anything that felt confusing or broken: use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight (take a screenshot first) or write support@tangomike.app.
