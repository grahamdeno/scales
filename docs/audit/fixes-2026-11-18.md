# Fixes for the 18 November 2026 review (Scales 2.37.0, App Store 1.1)

The review read commit 54aa181 and the live store and browser app, and listed 39 findings (G-1 to G-39). All are in
2.37.0. Checked by `tests/e2e/audit.e2e.js` where a browser can check them; the rest need a phone (marked).

The review's challenges to settled rules are answered in HANDOFF.md, Design rules: Stone stays; the deeper Dark sheet
is written into the rule; counts are totals; the gear and bookmark are the named exceptions to "actions are words".
