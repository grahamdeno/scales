How Scales works

Swipe up to move on.

Pull down to put a thought away.
It waits in Your thoughts.

Tap to edit it or add a note.
Undo lasts five seconds after any move.

How long you stay decides when it comes back.

20 seconds or less: back tomorrow, a little bolder.
30 seconds or more: it rests longer each time.
An edit or a note: it rests longest.

Nothing leaves unless you put it away.
