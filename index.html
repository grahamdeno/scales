How it works

Not today raises a principle's weight, so what you set aside comes back more often, up to four times as often as a new one. Still working on it, after at least 8 seconds on the card, lowers that weight by one step. Living it retires the principle to your record.

Origin

The name comes from scales falling from the eyes, and from Nietzsche's three metamorphoses: the camel carries ideas, the lion tests them, the child acts from them without thinking. Scales is built for the lion stage.
