Use AI in Scales?

To write a question for each thought, pick the quote that fits it best, and have today’s voice answer your replies, Scales sends these, through its own server, to Anthropic, an AI company:

  • the thought on screen and how many times you have skipped it
  • the quotes it is choosing between
  • what you write in Reply

Nothing else leaves your phone. Without AI, Scales uses questions written into the app. You can change this anytime in Settings > Your deck.

Notifications are off for Scales. Turn them on in iPhone Settings > Notifications > Scales.

Set up a reminder with Shortcuts
1In Shortcuts: Automation, New Automation, Time of Day. Pick a time, then Run Immediately.
2Add the Show Notification action with the words: Scales, today’s set.
3Repeat after 6 PM with: Scales, saved for later. Open Scales from the Home Screen when it arrives.

A link would open Safari, which keeps its own copy of the data, so the reminder points you to the Home Screen app instead.

How Scales works
  • One thought at a time. Today’s question shows the line Scales has for it.
  • Choose at the bottom of the screen: Skip, Reflect or Lived.
  • Reflect shows a real quote from today’s voice. After eight seconds it counts, and the thought gets lighter.
  • Skip rests a thought for a few days. It comes back first, set heavier, until you live it.
  • Later saves a thought for this evening, after 6 PM.
  • Lived moves it to your record in the Ledger, with where it showed up.
  • Reply to any line to keep what came up. With Scales Plus, today’s voice answers, and you can go back and forth.
  • Press and hold a thought to edit it, or a quote to keep it as a thought of its own.

About Scales

Version

The name comes from scales falling from the eyes, and from Nietzsche’s three metamorphoses: the camel carries ideas, the lion tests them, the child acts from them without thinking. Scales is built for the lion stage.

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Works offline once installed.
