Use AI in Scales?

To write a question for each principle, pick the quote that fits it best, and answer you in Talk it through, Scales sends these, through its own server, to Anthropic, an AI company:

  • the principle on screen and how many times you have skipped it
  • the quotes it is choosing between
  • what you write in Talk it through

Nothing else leaves your phone. Without AI, Scales uses questions written into the app. You can change this anytime in Settings > Advanced.

Set up a reminder with Shortcuts
1In Shortcuts: Automation, New Automation, Time of Day. Pick a time, then Run Immediately.
2Add the Show Notification action with the words: Scales, today’s set.
3Repeat after 6 PM with: Scales, saved for later. Open Scales from the Home Screen when it arrives.

A link would open Safari, which keeps its own copy of the data, so the reminder points you to the Home Screen app instead.

How Scales works
  • One principle at a time. Tap it to see the question Scales has for it.
  • Press and hold the bottom of the screen for your choices: Living it, Sit with it or Skip. Slide onto one, or let go and tap one.
  • Sit with it shows a real quote from today’s voice. After eight seconds it counts, and the principle gets lighter.
  • Skip rests a principle for a few days. It comes back first, set heavier, until you live it.
  • Later saves a principle for this evening, after 6 PM.
  • Living it moves it to your record in the Ledger, with where it showed up.
  • Press and hold a principle to edit it, or a quote to keep it as a principle of its own.

Version

The name comes from scales falling from the eyes, and from Nietzsche’s three metamorphoses: the camel carries ideas, the lion tests them, the child acts from them without thinking. Scales is built for the lion stage.
