Daily reminder

Scales has no server, so it cannot send notifications itself. An iPhone automation can remind you instead. Set it up once for the morning set and once for the evening check-in.

1In Shortcuts: Automation, New Automation, Time of Day. Pick a time, then Run Immediately.
2Add the Show Notification action with the words: Scales, today’s set.
3Repeat for the evening, after 6 PM: Scales, evening check-in. Open Scales from the Home Screen when it arrives.

A link would open Safari, which keeps its own copy of the data, so the reminder points you to the Home Screen app instead.

How it works

Hold the ring under a principle. The words ink in as you hold. Let go early and the app pushes back once with a line to reconsider: hold again to stay with it, or set it aside. Hold until the ring closes to test it: you get a question to sit with, Talk it through with today's voice, and Next after eight seconds, when the test lowers its Not today count. Slide up while holding when you are living it. A quick tap does nothing. Press and hold the principle to edit it, or a written line to keep it. The heavier a principle is set, the more often you have set it aside. Show, in the menu, draws the deck from one collection. With a keyboard: the arrow keys for the three moves, N to capture, E to edit, L for the Ledger, M for the menu, comma for Settings.

About

Version

The name comes from scales falling from the eyes, and from Nietzsche's three metamorphoses: the camel carries ideas, the lion tests them, the child acts from them without thinking. Scales is built for the lion stage.

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Works offline once installed. After an update, close and reopen Scales; if the version has not changed, the phone is still running the previous one.
