Using Scales

What is a thought in Scales?

Anything you’d like to live by, or aren’t sure you should: a line from a book, something your father said, a rule you made after a bad week. One sentence is enough. Scales shows you a few each day so you can weigh them.

What do Skip, Reflect and Lived do?

Skip sets it aside for a few days and counts it; skip it three times and it moves to Avoiding in the Ledger. Reflect shows a real line from a thinker and asks you to sit with it. Lived means you acted on it without being reminded; it leaves the deck for your record.

What if I think a thought is wrong?

Use Question it. Scales lays out the strongest honest case against it, next to your own reasons. Then keep it, revise it, or let it go. Letting go is kept in your Ledger with your reason, because changing your mind is the point.

How many show up each day?

Five by default. Change it in Settings, Your deck, Thoughts a day. When you’re done, Scales says so and stops. Keep going if you want more.

Can I change the app icon?

Yes, in the iPhone app: Settings, App icon. Choose Black or Light.

Your data

Where are my thoughts kept?

On your phone. The iPhone app also saves everything to one file each day, in iCloud Drive, Scales (or Files, On My iPhone, Scales if iCloud is off).

How do I move to a new phone?

Install Scales, then Settings, Your data, Restore, and choose the latest Scales backup file from iCloud Drive. Restoring adds to what’s there; it never overwrites.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. Then remove the Scales folder in iCloud Drive if you don’t want the backups. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Scales Plus

What does Scales Plus add?

A question written for each thought, the quote that fits it best, voices that answer what you write, and the case against when you question a thought. $19.99 a year, with 14 days free.

How do I restore or cancel?

Settings, Scales Plus, Restore purchases. To cancel, choose Manage subscription there, or in the iPhone Settings app under your name, Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before it renews.

It says AI needs Scales Plus, but I have it.

Open Settings, Scales Plus, and tap Restore purchases. If it still says so, email us with the time it happened.
