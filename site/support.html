Using Scales

What do I put in Scales?

Anything on your mind that you want to come back to: a line from a book, something someone said, a rule you're trying to keep. One sentence is enough.

Where do the sets come from?

Franklin's Virtues, The Stoics and Know Yourself are lines from public-domain books, put into plain modern English. The original wording is kept and checked word for word. After Jung lines are written in his spirit, not his words. Questions to Sit With were written for Scales.

Can I bring in quotes I already have?

Yes. Open Your thoughts, tap the gear, and choose Import thoughts. Paste a list, or choose a text file, a CSV export or a Kindle "My Clippings.txt". A name after a dash becomes who said it. Five arrive each day.

How do I write a new thought?

Pull up Your thoughts at the bottom of the screen and tap New at the top.

How do I move between thoughts?

Swipe up to move on. Pull down to put a thought away. Tap it to edit it or add a note. After either move, Undo stays on screen for five seconds.

When does a thought come back?

A glance of 20 seconds or less brings it back in five days. Stay with it for 30 seconds or more and it rests longer each time. Edit it or add a note and it rests longest.

Why is one thought in bolder type?

You've glanced past it before, and each time sets it a little heavier. Staying with it lightens it again. A small line under it can also say how often you've passed it lately.

Where did a thought go?

Pull up Your thoughts at the bottom of the screen and tap Put away at the end of the list. Tap Bring back and it returns tomorrow.

Can I say why I put a thought away?

Yes, if you want to. After you pull a thought down, tap Say why in the message at the bottom and write a line. You can add or change it later from the Put away list. It shows in Insights.

How many thoughts show up each day?

Up to 30, chosen so all of your thoughts come round, not the same ones every day. Up to five you keep skipping are mixed in on purpose, new ones arrive a few a day, and the rest are the ones you haven't seen in longest. At 30 you can stop, or tap Keep going.

What are Insights?

A page in Settings that shows what's changing, counted on your phone from what you did: thoughts you used to skip and now stay with, what you've put away and why, how your wording changed, what you're still avoiding, and when you think. Each part fills in once there's enough to say something true.

How do I close Edit?

Tap Done, pull it down, or tap the thought showing above it. All three keep what you wrote. Cancel throws your changes away.

How do I delete a thought?

Tap it, then tap Delete twice. Tap anywhere else to cancel.

Can I change the colors?

Yes. Open Your thoughts, tap the gear, and under Look choose Colors: Pistachio, or Stone for warm grey and graphite.

Can I choose Light or Dark?

Scales follows your phone. To pick one, open Your thoughts, tap the gear, and under Look choose Automatic, Light or Dark.

Does Scales need a connection?

No. The iPhone app has everything inside it and works with no connection at all. In a browser, Scales needs a connection the first time, then opens without one.

Apple Intelligence

What does Apple Intelligence do in Scales?

Three things. Argue with it, in Edit, shows the strongest case against a thought and the belief it rests on. Tensions, in Your thoughts, finds pairs of your thoughts that pull against each other. Themes and your week, in Settings, groups your thoughts by theme. None of it tells you what to think.

Which iPhones can use it?

An iPhone 15 Pro or later, on iOS 26, with Apple Intelligence turned on in the iPhone's Settings. On other iPhones, and in the browser, these three don't appear and everything else works the same.

Are my thoughts sent anywhere for it?

No. It runs on Apple's on-device model, on your iPhone. Your thoughts aren't sent to Apple, to us, or to anyone.

A tension or an argument doesn't make sense. Why?

The on-device model is small and sometimes misses. An argument is made once for the words as they are; reword the thought and you can ask again. If two thoughts don't really pull against each other, open the pair and tap Not a tension. They won't be paired again.

Argue with it says "This one isn't for arguing with." Why?

Some thoughts are about safety, recovery or your own worth, and an argument against them could do harm, so Scales doesn't make one. If you're going through something hard, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number.

Your data

Where are my thoughts kept?

On your phone. The iPhone app also saves everything each time you leave it to iCloud Drive, Scales (or Files, On My iPhone, Scales when iCloud is off), and keeps the last 14 copies.

Can I make a backup myself?

You don't need to in the iPhone app, but you can. Open Your thoughts, tap the gear, choose Backups, then Back up now.

How do I move to a new phone?

Install Scales on the new phone. Open Your thoughts, tap the gear, choose Backups, then Restore from a backup, and pick the latest file in iCloud Drive, Scales. Restoring adds what's missing and never overwrites.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. Then remove the Scales folder in iCloud Drive if you don't want the backups. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
