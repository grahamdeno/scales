Using Scales

What do I put in Scales?

Anything on your mind that you want to come back to: a line from a book, something someone said, a rule you're trying to keep. One sentence is enough.

How do I move between thoughts?

Swipe up to move on. Pull down to put a thought away. Tap it to edit it or add a note. After either move, Undo stays on screen for five seconds.

Why did a thought come back so soon?

A glance of 20 seconds or less brings it back the next day. Stay with it for 30 seconds or more and it rests longer each time. Edit it or add a note and it rests longest.

Why is one thought in bolder type?

You've glanced past it before. Each time sets it a little heavier and moves it toward the front. Staying with it lightens it again.

Where did a thought go?

Pull up Your thoughts at the bottom of the screen and choose Put away. Tap Bring back and it returns tomorrow.

How many thoughts show up each day?

Every thought that's due. When none are left, Scales says when the next one is back.

How do I delete a thought?

Tap it, then tap Delete twice. Tap anywhere else to cancel.

Your data

Where are my thoughts kept?

On your phone. The iPhone app also saves everything once a day, and when you leave it, to iCloud Drive, Scales (or Files, On My iPhone, Scales when iCloud is off).

Can I make a backup myself?

Yes. Open Your thoughts, tap the gear, and choose Back up.

How do I move to a new phone?

Install Scales on the new phone. Open Your thoughts, tap the gear, choose Restore from a backup, and pick the latest file in iCloud Drive, Scales. Restoring adds what's missing and never overwrites.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. Then remove the Scales folder in iCloud Drive if you don't want the backups. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Still have a question?
