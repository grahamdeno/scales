How it works

Two moves. That's all.

Stay with a thought and it rests longer. Glance past it and it comes back a little bolder. Every one comes round in time.

Think it through

Your own thoughts are the hardest to question.

A thought you hold feels true from the inside. Questioning it is hard for three reasons.

You can't see your own assumptions.A thought feels obviously true, so the belief underneath it goes unnoticed.
You argue against yourself badly.Your counterarguments come out weak, because part of you doesn't want them to win.
You can't hold every thought at once.With dozens or hundreds of them, you never notice that two disagree, or which one you keep avoiding.

Scales helps with each, using Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. It argues the other side and points out what you keep missing. It never tells you what to think, and nothing leaves your phone.

Argue with it

Hear the other side.

Tap a thought, then Argue with it. You get the strongest honest case against it, in two or three sentences, and the one belief it quietly rests on.

No score and no advice. If the assumption is worth remembering, add it to your note.

Tensions

See where your thoughts disagree.

Each day Scales reads through some of your thoughts and finds pairs that pull against each other. Open one and both fill the screen, with "and yet" between them.

It doesn't pick a side. It puts the two in front of you, and the thinking is yours.

Argue with it and Tensions need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later, iOS 26). Everything else works on any iPhone and in your browser.

Insights

See what's changing.

Counted on your phone from what you actually did: thoughts you used to skip and now stay with, the ones you've put away and why, how your wording has changed, and what you're still avoiding.

No streaks and no scores. Just what's true, when there's enough to say it.

Your thoughts

Nothing disappears on its own.

Pull up the strip at the bottom to find anything. The last few you saw come first, then everything by where you stand with it: avoiding, sitting with, seen, and not met yet.

Put a thought away and you can say why, in a line. It waits there, one tap from coming back.
