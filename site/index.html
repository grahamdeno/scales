How it works

Two moves. That's all.

  • Swipe up. Move on. It comes back another day.
  • Pull down. Put it away. It waits in your list, one tap from coming back.
  • Tap. Edit it, or add a note about what came up.

Changed your mind? Undo stays on screen for five seconds.

No buttons to judge it

Scales reads the pause.

There's no rating, no streak and no "done". Scales goes by one thing: how long a thought stays on your screen.

A glance

20 seconds or less. It comes back tomorrow, a little bolder.

A real look

30 seconds or more. It rests longer each time.

An edit or a note

It rests longest.

The ones you keep glancing past come first, in heavier type, so they don't quietly slip away.

Your thoughts

Nothing disappears on its own.

Everything you've written is in Your thoughts, the strip at the bottom of the screen. Pull it up to search, see what's coming back and when, and bring back anything you put away.
