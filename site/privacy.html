Privacy.

Short version: everything you write stays on your phone. There's no account, no AI, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Everything you write: your thoughts, your notes, and when each one comes back. It's stored in the app on your device. The iPhone app saves a copy once a day to iCloud Drive, Scales, which is your iCloud, not ours. We can't see it.

What leaves your phone

Nothing you write. Opening Scales loads the app from scales.tangomike.app, like opening any website; the hosting services see that request, not your thoughts. Scales sends no reminders and makes no other requests.

What we never do

No ads. No analytics or tracking across apps and websites. No selling or sharing data. No account, so we hold nothing about you.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything on your phone, and the Scales folder in iCloud Drive to remove the backups. Because we keep nothing, there's nothing to request from us.
