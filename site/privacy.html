Privacy.

Short version: your thoughts live on your phone. Nothing is sold, nothing tracks you, and there is no account.

On your phoneYour thoughts, replies and Ledger. Saved daily to your own iCloud Drive.
Only with PlusOne thought at a time goes to our AI server to write its question. Nothing is kept.
NeverAds, tracking, selling data, or an account.

What stays on your phone

Everything you write: your thoughts, collections, replies, notes on where you lived one, and your Ledger. It’s stored in the app on your device. The iPhone app saves a copy once a day to iCloud Drive, Scales, which is your iCloud, not ours. We can’t see it.

What leaves your phone, and when

  • Quotes. When you Reflect, Scales looks up quotes by the day’s thinker on Wikiquote. Wikiquote sees the request like any website visit. Your thought is not sent.
  • Scales Plus AI. With Plus, the thought on screen (and your reply, when you write one) goes to our server at scales.tangomike.app, which passes it to Anthropic’s Claude to write a line, then returns it. Our server doesn’t store or log the text. Anthropic doesn’t use it to train models.
  • Your subscription. To confirm you have Plus, the app sends Apple’s signed subscription record with each AI request. It says you subscribed; it doesn’t say who you are.
  • Reminders. Scheduled on your phone. No server sends them.

What we never do

No ads. No analytics or tracking across apps and websites. No selling or sharing data. No account, so there’s nothing about you on our side to leak.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything on your phone, and the Scales folder in iCloud Drive to remove the backups. Because we keep nothing, there’s nothing to request from us.

Children

Scales isn’t made for children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes and contact

If this changes, the date below changes and the app will say so. Questions: support@tangomike.app.
