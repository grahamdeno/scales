Privacy.

Short version: everything you write stays on your phone. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and the Apple Intelligence features run on your iPhone, not on a server.

What stays on your phone

Everything you write: your thoughts, your notes, the reasons you give for putting one away, and when each one comes back. It's stored in the app on your device; in a browser, it's stored in that browser. The iPhone app saves a copy to iCloud Drive, Scales, each time you leave it. That's your iCloud, not ours. We can't see it.

Insights are counted on your phone from that same record. Nothing is sent to work them out.

Apple Intelligence

On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, Scales can argue with a thought, find tensions between your thoughts, and sort them into themes. All of it runs on Apple's on-device model, on your iPhone. Your thoughts are not sent to Apple, to us, or to anyone else to do it. What it writes is kept on your phone with the thought.

Scales also keeps a record, on your phone, of how it's used: which screens you open, which buttons you tap, and how long a thought stays on screen. It never holds your words. It goes into your backup so you can see it or share it if you choose, and it's never sent to us.

What leaves your phone

Nothing you write. The iPhone app has everything inside it and makes no requests over the internet. In a browser, opening Scales loads it from scales.tangomike.app, like opening any website: Cloudflare, which hosts it, sees that request (your IP address and the kind of device), not your thoughts. Scales sends no reminders.

What we never do

No ads. No analytics sent anywhere, and no tracking across apps and websites. No selling or sharing data. No account, so we hold nothing about you.

If you email us

If you write to support@tangomike.app, we get your email address and what you wrote. We use them only to answer you, and we'll delete the thread if you ask.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything on your phone, and the Scales folder in iCloud Drive to remove the backups. Apart from any email you send us, we keep nothing, so there's nothing else to request.
